## What is attributed to whom

- **Naval's point** sections paraphrase the book and cite pages. Short phrases in quotation marks are the only direct quotes.
- **Use when / Questions / Misleads when / Apply** sections and the selection map in `SKILL.md` are working guidance written for this skill. They are not Naval's claims.
- The "Deepens" column in the reading list is this skill's suggestion.
- "Latticework of mental models" is Charlie Munger's phrase, used as the skill's name.
