# Adding models

Use this when the user wants to add models from another book, essay, or their own experience.

## Rules

1. **One model, one entry.** Add it to `models.md` (a decision or analysis tool) or `thinking-principles.md` (a habit of mind).
2. **Paraphrase; cite the location.** Write the source's point in your own words with page or chapter numbers. Keep any direct quote very short. Do not paste book text.
3. **Do not inflate the source.** If the author gives one sentence, the "point" section is one or two sentences. Put your expansion under the working-guidance headings.
4. **Every model needs a "Misleads when".** A model without known limits gets misapplied.
5. **Update the selection map** in `SKILL.md` if the model fits a situation better than the existing ones. Keep the map short; merge rows rather than adding many.
6. **Update `sources.md`** with the source, its page map, and its link if an official one exists.
7. **Check for duplicates.** Many books name the same model (for example, inversion appears in Munger and Naval). Add the new source to the existing entry instead of creating a second one.

## Template

```markdown
## <Model name>

**<Author>'s point (<source short name>, p. N).** <Paraphrase of what the source actually says.>

**Use when** <situations where this model can change the conclusion>.

**Questions**
- <Concrete question the model forces>
- <Concrete question>
- <Concrete question>

**Misleads when** <the limits, the scope the author intended, and common misuse>.
```
